WHY THIS PROJECT?

A formulation designed around the patient.

Tuberculosis remains a major global health concern, with children representing a significant proportion of those affected.

Paediatric patients can experience challenges associated with the administration and acceptability of tuberculosis treatment.

Our project explores how pharmaceutical formulation can help address these practical challenges while supporting accurate and practical dosing.

01

OUR AIM

Developing an appropriate paediatric dosage form.

The aim of this project is to develop an appropriate pharmaceutical dosage form for the treatment of paediatric tuberculosis that addresses challenges associated with administration and acceptability.

The proposed formulation aims to provide accurate and practical dosing while improving ease of administration and acceptability.
